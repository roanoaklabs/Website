🚂 Rail Tracks

Connect the two stations with a single railroad line

How to Play

• The two station cells (marked with a dot) are the start and finish of the line.

• Click any other cell to mark it ✕ (no track) or place a straight/curved track piece.

• The numbers above each column and beside each row show exactly how many cells in that line contain track.

• The finished track must form one unbroken line from station to station — no branches, no extra loops.
